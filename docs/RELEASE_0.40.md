# 0.40 · 41 款 Photoshop 原生滤镜

**[下载 Windows x64 安装包](https://github.com/Three-LEI/AE-to-PS-Plugins/releases/download/v0.40/AE-to-PS-Plugins-0.40-Windows-x64.zip)**

- **新增 10 款**。多色调、镜像、重复拼贴、综合杂色、球面贴图、圆柱贴图、雨雪模拟、通道模糊、复合模糊和像素排序。
- **预览更大**。默认预览区为 360 × 360 逻辑像素。适用的效果可以用左键在预览中拖动位置和方向。
- **新增贴花和渐变控制**。球面与圆柱可以用同一图层制作底材和贴花。复合模糊可以用参考图层或内置渐变控制模糊范围。
- **分类更清楚**。滤镜归入九个 `ZaYu-` 分组。

退出 Photoshop，把所需的 `.8bf` 放入 `Plug-ins` 目录后重启。需要再次编辑参数时，先将图层转为智能对象。

本机验证覆盖 PS2020 和 PS2026。Win7／CC2018 待实机复测。 [效果清单](https://github.com/Three-LEI/AE-to-PS-Plugins/blob/v0.40/docs/EFFECTS.md) · [图集](https://github.com/Three-LEI/AE-to-PS-Plugins/blob/v0.40/docs/GALLERY.md) · [验证范围](https://github.com/Three-LEI/AE-to-PS-Plugins/blob/v0.40/docs/VALIDATION.md)

## English

Version 0.40 adds Toner, Mirror, RepeTile, Noise, Sphere, Cylinder, Rain & Snow, Channel Blur, Compound Blur, and Pixel Sort. The 41 filters share a larger preview and nine `ZaYu-` menu groups. Where supported, drag position and direction handles directly in the preview. Sphere and Cylinder support same-layer decals, and Compound Blur accepts a built-in gradient or a reference layer.

Download the Windows x64 plug-in ZIP above, quit Photoshop, and copy the required `.8bf` files into its `Plug-ins` directory. Tested in PS2020 and PS2026. Windows 7 with CC2018 still needs a retest.
